What this does. Capture a lawn boundary and any no-go zones, then export them in the exact frame the ZTR coverage planner reads.

1 · Draw the boundary. Walk the edge and tap Add point at each corner (GPS Walk), or switch to Tap map and place corners over the satellite image. Tap Close boundary when done.
2 · Add no-go zones (optional). Tap + No-go zone and outline anything the mower must avoid — a tree, flower bed, pond. These become holes in the plan.
3 · Export. lawn.yaml drops straight into the planner; GeoJSON keeps true lat/lon; Local XY is the flat metric frame.

Satellite is a backdrop, not the source of truth. Consumer GPS is ~3–5 m and aerial imagery can sit several metres off. For the real machine, capture with the ZTR's RTK receiver — the same export works, just far more precise.
